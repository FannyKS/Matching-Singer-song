🎤 Matching-Singer-song

Match every song to the singer who made it famous.

How to play

  • Each round deals out 10 songs at random in Easy, 15 in Advance — every song has exactly one correct singer.
  • Pick a song card and it flips over to reveal the title.
  • Then choose the singer who performs it. A singer with several songs gets a card for each.
  • Correct matches glow green; wrong guesses shake and flip back. Match all 10 to finish the round.
  • You're scored on first-try matches, total attempts, and time — with a score card at the end. 🎶
